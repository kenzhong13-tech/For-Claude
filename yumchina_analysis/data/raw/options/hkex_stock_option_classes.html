                 Stock Options


                Item
                Contract Terms
                Underlying Stocks & HKATS Codes
                		 Please refer to the list of stock options
                Option Types
                		 Puts and calls
                Contract Size
                		 Relevant information regarding contract size and tier level of individual stock option classes can be found in the list of stock options
                Contracted Value Option Premium multiplied by the Contract Size
                Expiries             

                Monthly expiries: Spot, the next three calendar months and the next three calendar quarter months

                (Monthly expiries will be introduced for all option classes traded on the Exchange. 
                The Exchange may introduce any other longer-dated expiry month in selected stock option classes as it deems necessary)

                 

                Weekly expiries: Spot and the next week
                provided that no weekly contracts will be listed if such weekly contracts would expire on the same day as the expiry day for the spot month contract.

                (The Exchange may from time to time introduce weekly expiries for such option classes as it considers appropriate)
