LATEST BUSINESS UPDATE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2024

This  announcement  is  made  by  the  board  of  directors  (the  “Board”)  of  Pop  Mart  International 
Group Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries and consolidated affiliated entities, 
the  “Group”)  on  a  voluntary  basis  to  inform  the  shareholders  of  the  Company  and  potential 
investors about the latest business update of the Group.

Overall Revenue Performance in the Third Quarter of 2024

Overall  revenue  (unaudited)  for  the  third  quarter  of  2024  recorded  a  period-on-period  increase 
of  120%-125%  as  compared  to  the  third  quarter  of  2023,  among  which,  revenue  attributed  from 
operations  in  Mainland  China  recorded  a  period-on-period  increase  of  55%-60%  and  revenue 
attributed from operations in Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and overseas recorded a period-on-period 
increase of 440%-445%.

Revenue  Performance  by  Each  Respective  Channel  of  Operations  in  the  Mainland  China  in 
the Third Quarter of 2024

The revenue (unaudited) attributed from each respective channel of the operations in the Mainland 
China for the third quarter of 2024 as compared to the third quarter of 2023 is as follows:

(i) 

for retail stores operations, a period-on-period increase of 30%-35% was recorded;

(ii) 

for roboshops operations, a period-on-period increase of 20%-25% was recorded;

(iii)  for Pop Draw operations, a period-on-period increase of 55%-60% was recorded;

(iv)  for  the  operations  of  e-commerce  platforms  and  other  online  platforms,  a  period-on-period 
increase of 135%-140% was recorded, of which the operations for Douyin platform recorded 
a  period-on-period  increase  of  115%-120%  and  the  operations  for  Tmall  flagship  store 
recorded a period-on-period increase of 155%-160%; and

(v) 

for the operations in wholesales and other channels, a period-on-period increase of 45%-50% 
was recorded.
