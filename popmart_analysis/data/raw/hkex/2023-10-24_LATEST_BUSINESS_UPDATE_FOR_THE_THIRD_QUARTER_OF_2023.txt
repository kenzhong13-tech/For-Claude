LATEST BUSINESS UPDATE
FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2023

This  announcement  is  made  by  the  board  of  directors  (the  “Board”)  of  Pop  Mart  International 
Group Limited (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries and consolidated affiliated entities, 
the  “Group”)  on  a  voluntary  basis  to  inform  the  shareholders  of  the  Company  and  potential 
investors about the latest business update of the Group.

Overall Revenue Performance in the Third Quarter of 2023

Overall  revenue  (unaudited)  for  the  third  quarter  of  2023  recorded  a  period-on-period  increase 
of  35%-40%  as  compared  to  the  third  quarter  of  2022,  among  which,  revenue  attributed  from 
operations  in  Mainland  China  recorded  a  period-on-period  increase  of  25%-30%  and  revenue 
attributed from operations in Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and overseas recorded a period-on-period 
increase of 120%-125%.

Revenue Performance by Channel of Mainland China in the Third Quarter of 2023

The revenue (unaudited) attributed from each respective channel of the operations in the Mainland 
China for the third quarter of 2023 as compared to the third quarter of 2022 is as follows:

(i) 

for retail stores operations, a period-on-period increase of 35%-40% was recorded;

(ii) 

for roboshops operations, a period-on-period increase of 45%-50% was recorded;

(iii)  for Pop Draw operations, a period-on-period decrease of 10%-15% was recorded;

(iv)  for  the  operations  of  e-commerce  platforms  and  other  online  platforms,  a  period-on-period 
increase  of  20%-25%  was  recorded,  of  which  the  operations  for  Tik-Tok  flagship  store 
recorded  a  period-on-period  increase  of  875%-880%  and  the  operations  for  Tmall  flagship 
store recorded a period-on-period decrease of 20%-25%; and

(v) 

for the operations in wholesales and other channels, a period-on-period increase of 50%-55% 
was recorded.
