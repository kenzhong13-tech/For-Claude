                              VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT

            CONVENING OF 2026 THIRD QUARTERLY RESULTS BRIEFING

Ping An Insurance (Group) Company of China, Ltd. (the “Company”) proposes to publish its 2026
third quarterly results on the website of The Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited
(www.hkexnews.hk) on October 28, 2026. In order to enable investors to have a deeper and more
comprehensive understanding of the 2026 third quarterly results and the business operation of the
Company, the Company proposes to convene the 2026 third quarterly results briefing (the “Results
Briefing”) through online text interaction from 18:45 p.m. to 19:45 p.m. on October 28, 2026.
Senior management members of the Company will attend the Results Briefing.

Investors may put forward relevant questions by sending emails to the Company's investor relations
email box: IR@pingan.com.cn by 23:59 p.m. on October 23, 2026, and the common concerned
questions will be answered by the Company at the Results Briefing. Investors may visit the website
of SSE Roadshow Center (http://roadshow.sseinfo.com) to attend the Results Briefing online from
18:45 p.m. to 19:45 p.m. on October 28, 2026.

After the Results Briefing, investors may visit the website of SSE Roadshow Center
(http://roadshow.sseinfo.com) to view the briefing and main contents of the Results Briefing.
