Phase III clinical study of 3SBio’s recombinant human thrombopoietin (rhTPO) injection 
(TPIAO®) in pediatric ITP indication achieved the pre-defined primary endpoint

Shenyang, China, 10 May 2022

3SBio Inc. (01530.HK) (“3SBio” or “Company”), a leading biopharmaceutical company in China,  
announced  today  that  a  multicenter,  randomized,  double-blind,  placebo-controlled  study  on  the 
safety,  efficacy,  and  pharmacokinetics  of  recombinant  human  thrombopoietin  (rhTPO)  injection 
(TPIAO®)  in  children  or  adolescents  with  chronic  primary  immune  thrombocytopenia  (ITP) 
achieved the pre-defined primary endpoint.

The  main  efficacy  index  of  phase  III  clinical  trial  is  the  overall  efficacy.  The  statistical  analysis 
results  show  that  the  experimental  group  is  superior  to  the  control  group  and  there  is  a  statistical 
difference in the overall efficacy between two groups (P = 0.0029). There were no serious adverse 
events  related  to  the  study  drug  or  any  suspected  unexpected  serious  adverse  reactions  during  the 
study. The Company plans to submit the New Drug Application to the National Medical Products 
Administration in the near future.

Primary  immune  thrombocytopenia  (ITP)  is  an  acquired  autoimmune  hemorrhagic  disease.  Its 
treatment  follows  the  principle  of  individualization  and  patients  are  encouraged  to  participate  in 
treatment  decision-making.  On  the  basis  of  minimizing  adverse  reactions  during  treatment,  the 
platelet  count  is  elevated  to  a  safe  level  to  minimize  bleeding  cases.  In  case  the  first-line  therapy 
such  as  glucocorticoids  or  intravenous  immune  globulin  is  ineffective  and  there  is  still  a  need  for 
treatment, the second-line therapy would be applied. Thrombopoietin like TPIAO® is the preferred 
drug for second-line therapy recommended by Chinese ITP guidelines.

TPIAO® is developed in-house by 3SBio and has been launched for more than ten years, and it has 
been verified by previous studies for its good efficacy and safety for treatment of adult ITP. In order 
to meet the demand for clinical treatment of children with ITP and respond to the call of the country 
for  development  of  application  drug  for  children,  3SBio  has  initiated  the  launching  of  phase  III 
clinical study for children ITP indications in a total of 10 children’s hospitals and general hospitals 
across the country.

Dr.  LOU  Jing,  Chairman  of  3SBio,  said:  “A  clinical  trial  with  children  as  the  subjects  encounters 
more difficulties and challenges when compared to clinical trials with adult subjects. We are very 
glad  that  the  phase  III  clinical  trial  of  TPIAO®  for  children’s  ITP  indications  has  been  completed 
successfully  and  achieved  the  pre-defined  endpoint.  This  study  provides  fuller  evidence  of  the 
efficacy  and safety for the use of TPIAO® in the treatment of pediatric patients. If the application 
of TPIAO® for children’s ITP indications is approved, its availability in pediatric clinical treatment 
will  be  enhanced,  and  the  demand  for  clinical  treatments  of  children  ITP  patients  can  be  better 
satisfied  in  our  country.  We,  at  3SBio,  are  always  committed  to  the  research  and  development  of 
high-quality  biopharmaceutical  products,  and  at  the  same  time,  we  also  proactively  explore  and 
widen the indications of products already on the market with a view to benefitting more patients.”
