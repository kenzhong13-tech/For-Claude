SHENYANG SUNSHINE HAS ENTERED INTO
A COOPERATION AGREEMENT
WITH HAIHE BIOPHARMA IN RESPECT OF
PACLITAXEL ORAL SOLUTION

Shenyang  Sunshine  Pharmaceutical  Co.,  Ltd.  (“Shenyang  Sunshine”),  a  subsidiary  of  3SBio 
Inc.  (the  “Company”),  entered  into  the  Paclitaxel  Oral  Solution  Exclusive  Commercialization 
Cooperation  Agreement  (the  “Agreement”)  with  Shanghai  Haihe  Biopharma  Research  and 
Development  Co.,  Ltd.  (“Haihe  Biopharma”)  and  its  subsidiary  Shanghai  RMX  Biopharma 
Co., Ltd. (“RMX Biopharma”). Pursuant to the Agreement, Shenyang Sunshine will obtain the 
exclusive  commercialization  rights  of  RMX  Biopharma’s  product  of  Paclitaxel  Oral  Solution 
in  Mainland  China  and  Hong  Kong.  Shenyang  Sunshine  will  pay  RMX  Biopharma  an  initial 
payment  as  well  as  research  and  development  and  sales  milestone  payments  in  accordance 
with the Agreement. RMX Biopharma will continue to be responsible for the ongoing phase III 
clinical trials and any follow up development, clinical and registration works for the product.

This is a voluntary announcement made by the Company. There is no assurance that the product 
will  eventually  be  commercialized  successfully.  Shareholders  and  potential  investors  of  the 
Company are advised to exercise caution when dealing in the shares of the Company.
