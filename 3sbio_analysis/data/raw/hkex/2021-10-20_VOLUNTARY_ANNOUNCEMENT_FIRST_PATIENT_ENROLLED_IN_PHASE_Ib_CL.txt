the United States. Based on the results of the study, the product is well-tolerated and safe, and the 
pharmacokinetic characteristics suggests a favorable druggability.

Atopic  Dermatitis  (AD)  is  a  chronic,  recurrent  and  inflammatory  skin  disease.  Patients  with 
moderate  to  severe  atopic  dermatitis  generally  have  rashes  on  majority  of  their  skin,  leading  to 
symptoms such as strong and lasting pruritus, xerosis cutis, incrustation and exudation. With very 
limited therapy drugs, the quality of life of patients have been significantly impacted. In the past 30 
years,  the  incidence  of  atopic  dermatitis  globally  increased  gradually,  and  the  incidence  of  atopic 
dermatitis for children aged between 1 and 7 in China reaching 12.94% while that for infants of 1 to 
12 months old attaining 30.48%. Atopic dermatitis is an auto-immune disease arose from abnormal 
immune  responses  and  the  major  cause  involve  different  sessions  of  the  immune  system,  such  as 
presenting  of  allergen  by  Langerhans  cells  and  dendritic  cells,  abnormal  immune  responses  that 
is mainly Th2, dysfunction of regulatory T cells, IgE overproduction and increase in eosinophilia. 
Currently,  corticosteroid  drugs  are  the  most  commonly  used  drugs  for  treating  such  disease; 
however, the serious side effects of these drugs restricted the application. Therefore, there remains a 
wide range of unmet clinical needs for such disease.

IL-4Rα  is  part  of  the  signaling  complex  of  interleukin  4  (IL-4)  and  interleukin  13  (IL-13)  and 
plays  a  key  role  in  the  pathogenesis  of  atopic  dermatitis.  611  can  regulate  the  immune  function 
by  inhibiting  IL-4Rα  and  blocking  the  signaling  of  IL-4  and  IL-13,  including  inhibiting  Th2  cell 
differentiation, alleviating respiratory allergic reactions and IgE synthesis, so as to alleviate diseases 
such as atopic dermatitis. Dupilumab (trade name: Dupixent) developed by Regeneron/Sanofi is the 
only  marketed  drug  targeting  the  interleukin  4  receptor  (IL-4R)  worldwide.  Approved  indications 
include moderate to severe atopic dermatitis, asthma and sinusitis cause by nasal polyps. Based on 
the  completed  research  results,  the  action  mechanism  of  611  and  the  preclinical  and  clinical  trial 
data  are  highly  similar  to  Dupilumab,  therefore,  such  product  has  the  potential  to  compete  in  the 
international market.

About 611

611  is  a  humanized  anti-IL-4Rα  monoclonal  antibody  with  an  independent  intellectual  property 
right  that  is  innovatively  developed  by  Sunshine  Guojian.  611  has  a  completely  new  amino  acid 
sequence, and can regulate the immune function and alleviate diseases such as atopic dermatitis by 
specifically binding to IL-4Rα and blocking the signaling of IL-4 and IL-13.
