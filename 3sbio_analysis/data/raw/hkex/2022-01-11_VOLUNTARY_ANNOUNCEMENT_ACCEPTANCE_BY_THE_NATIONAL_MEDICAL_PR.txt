THE SUBMISSION AND ACCEPTANCE OF THE APPLICATION FOR THE MARKET 
LAUNCH OF 5% MINOXIDIL FOAM OF 3SBIO INC.

(SHANGHAI,  China,  January  11,  2022)  3SBio  Inc.  (01530.HK),  announced  today  that  the 
application  for  the  market  launch  of  5%  Minoxidil  Foam  submitted  to  the  National  Medical 
Products  Administration  (NMPA)  of  the  PRC  was  accepted  for  the  treatment  of  androgenetic 
alopecia.

5%  Minoxidil  Foam  is  the  new-generation  anti-hair  loss  and  hair  growth  product  of  3SBio  Inc. 
based on Minoxidil Tincture (Mandi), which, being the only domestic minoxidil foam with clinical 
studies  and  application  for  the  market  launch,  is  expected  to  be  the  first  domestic  minoxidil  foam 
approved for the market launch. Its prescription does not contain propylene glycol, to which a few 
patients are allergic, and thus can lower the occurrence rate of allergy pruritus. Compared with other 
topical preparation, such as tincture, liniment, topical solution and spray, foam is easier to distribute 
on  epidermis  as  it  has  a  lower  density,  allowing  the  even  distribution  of  medicine  after  smearing. 
Meanwhile, the dosing of foam is more precise, which can prevent the stimulation of hair growth at 
unexpected part, facilitate the effective positioning treatment for patients’ hair loss at involved area 
and provide better compliance and experience to patients. 

The  application  for  the  market  launch  was  based  on  a  multi-centered,  double-blind,  randomized 
controlled  clinical  trial  of  the  efficacy  on  patients  with  androgenetic  alopecia  to  assess  5% 
Minoxidil  Foam  and  ROGAINE®  (5%  Minoxidil  Foam)  in  terms  of  their  equivalence,  safety  and 
tolerability.  The  experimental  result  reached  the  preset  statistical  end  point,  indicating  that  in  this 
study, the efficacy of the trial medicine, 5% Minoxidil Foam, is equivalent to that of the controlled 
medicine,  ROGAINE®.  There  is  similarity  between  5%  Minoxidil  Foam  and  ROGAINE®  in  terms 
of safety and tolerability.

Dr.  LOU  Jing,  the  chairman  of  3SBio  Inc.,  commented,  “We  are  glad  that  the  submission  of 
application for the market launch of 5% Minoxidil Foam was accepted. Androgenetic alopecia is the 
most common illness in relation to balding and there is a large population of androgenetic alopecia 
patients in China. 3SBio Inc. hopes to provide better treatment to androgenetic alopecia patients in 
China  through  the  development  of  the  product  and  continue  to  consolidate  the  leading  position  of 
3SBio Inc. in the area of topical drugs for treating hair loss.”
