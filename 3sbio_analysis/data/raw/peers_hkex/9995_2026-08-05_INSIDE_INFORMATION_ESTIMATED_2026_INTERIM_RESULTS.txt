III.  MAIN REASONS FOR THE CHANGE IN RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD

During  the  2026  Interim  Period,  the  sales  revenue  from  the  Company’s  core  products, 
telitacicept  and  disitamab  vedotin,  has  continued  to  grow  in  the  PRC;  meanwhile,  the 
Company  has  entered  into  an  exclusive  licensing  agreement  with  an  AbbVie  controlled 
company  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  “AbbVie”)  regarding  RC148,  pursuant  to  which  AbbVie 
will  obtain  the  exclusive  rights  to  develop,  manufacture,  and  commercialize  RC148  outside 
the  Greater  China  region,  leading  to  a  significant  increase  in  technology  licensing  revenue, 
and it is expected to record a profit for the first half of 2026.
